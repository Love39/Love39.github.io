Retail Sales Performance Analysis

Overview

Analyzed historical cafe and retail transactional data using SQL and Excel to uncover revenue drivers, evaluate category velocity, and optimize weekly sales schedules.


Key Findings & Actionable Insights

  • Identifies that the Food category serves as the primary revenue engine; recommmended expanding inventory on top-selling food items to capture unmet demand.
  • Diagnose Mid-Week Slumps: Uncovered consistent, below-average revenue drops on specific mid-week days, revealing opportunities for targeted loyalty promotions to smooth out daily sales flunctuations.
  • Optimize Cross-Category Merchandising: Isolated distinct purchasing patterns where sales trends diverged across categories, providing a data-backed blueprint for strategic menu pairing and bundle pricing.

Employee Attrition Analysis Using Excel

Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to evaluate revenue trends and identify high-performing product categories.


Key findings

  • The overall employee attrition rate was 16.12%, indicating that approximately one in six employees left the organization.
  • The Sales department recorded the highest number of attrition rate of 20.63% during the period analyzed.
  • Among the job roles, Laboratory Technicians experienced the highest turnover, accounting for 62 employee exits.
  • Employees who worked overtime showed significantly higher attrition, with 53.59% of departing employees having worked overtime, suggesting workload may contribute to turnover.
  • Attrition varied across job satisfaction levels, indicating that employee satisfaction may influence retention outcomes.
