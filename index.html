Retail Sales Performance Analysis

Overview

Analyzed historical cafe and retail transactional data using SQL and Excel to uncover revenue drivers, evaluate category velocity, and optimize weekly sales schedules.


Key Findings & Actionable Insights

  • Identifies that the Food category serves as the primary revenue engine; recommmended expanding inventory on top-selling food items to capture unmet demand.
  • Diagnose Mid-Week Slumps: Uncovered consistent, below-average revenue drops on specific mid-week days, revealing opportunities for targeted loyalty promotions to smooth out daily sales flunctuations.
  • Optimize Cross-Category Merchandising: Isolated distinct purchasing patterns where sales trends diverged across categories, providing a data-backed blueprint for strategic menu pairing and bundle pricing.

Tools Used

  • SQL (SQLite): Advanced data querying, aggregation, and performance trend calculation.
  • Microsoft Excel: Pivot tables, dynamic column charts, and dashboard formatting.

Employee Attrition Analysis

Overview

Developed an interactive HR dashboard in Excel to analyze employee turnover patterns, isolating the key operational drivers behind staff departures to improve corporate retention strategies.


Key Findings & Actionable Insights

  • Address the Overtime Burnout Link: Discovered that 53.59% of departing employees routinely worked overtime; recommended implementing structural workload caps and monitoring systems to mitigate burnout-driven turnover.
  • Target High-Risk Roles & Demographics: Isolated the highest attrition rates within the Sales Department (20.63%), specifically affecting employees aged 26 to 35, providing HR with a targeted demographic for tailored retention programs.
  • Optimize Compensation Strategies: Mapped attrition against salary tiers, revealing that low-to-mid income earners comprised the highest volume of departures; proposed structured career-pathing and salary benchmarking reviews.

  • Microsoft Excel
  • Pivot tables
  • Charts & Data Visualization
